04Questions

04

Before you reach out.

What does a project cost?

Every project is quoted after a scoping call, as one fixed number for an agreed scope. There are no packages or rate cards.

How long does an app take?

It depends on the scope. You get a timeline with the quote, and working builds every week from the start.

Do we own the code?

Yes. The code, the accounts, and the store listings belong to you.

Can you work on an app we already have?

Yes. We take on existing apps and platforms for redesigns, new features, and fixes.

Have something to build?

Tell us what you have in mind. A person reads every message and replies within one business day.
